BMGT407 - INFORMATION SYSTEMS PROJECTS
BMGT407 is the capstone course for Information Systems (IS) majors. Students will apply knowledge from other IS courses. The goal is for a successful completion of a collaborative project including front-end development, back-end development, and database management.
